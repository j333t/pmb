PlusMinusBang (PMB) is a plain-text reasoning notation. When a line starts with
one of these symbols followed by a space, read it as:

  +  a point in favour          -  a point against
  !  attention — note this, but it can give way
  *  a hard condition — a rule, law, regulation or contract term that cannot
     give way, whatever anyone decides
  ?  a genuine open question    ~  unvalidated uncertainty
  (no symbol)  neutral fact or context

The difference between `!` and `*` is whether arguing could change it. A
deadline someone could move is `!`. A regulation is `*`.

Intensity and weight, both optional:
  A repeated symbol means "more so", up to three: `!` `!!` `!!!`
  A number right after the symbol is a weight, 0 to 1, meaning how much the
  line should count: `+0.8 Cuts delivery time in half`
For + and -, the symbol carries the sign, so the token reads as a signed weight
between -1 and 1. `*` takes neither — a hard condition is binary.

Never convert between the two. `!!` is not 0.66. An unweighted line is
unweighted, NOT 0.5 — treating absent as middling invents a judgment the
author declined to make.


The symbol counts only as the first non-whitespace character of a line, and the
symbol token — including any repeats or weight — must be followed by a space.
"2 + 2", "-5%" and "-0.5% margin" are all ordinary text.

Indentation means "responds to the line above" — a `-` under a `+` is a
limitation of it, a `+` under a `-` is a mitigation, a `?` under a `*`
questions whether that condition is really fixed.

Symbols are semantic, not a scorecard. Do not tally `+` against `-` to reach a
verdict. A single `*` can end a decision on its own.

Dated blocks are a history, newest first. Older blocks are not errors to be
reconciled — they are the record of how the thinking changed. Never rewrite
them.
