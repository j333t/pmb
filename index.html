The Context Trap

Important decisions and complex reasoning happen largely in our heads. Even when we write things down, we lose context. We forget the tradeoffs we considered, the information that changed our minds, and how our thinking evolved.

When we look back weeks or months later, we remember our conclusion, but rarely the reasoning that led there.

PlusMinusBang (PMB) is a low-friction method for quick capture & clear comprehension.

Common Questions

Seems like more work?

Nope, helps you think faster and clearer. Also reduces repeated explanations in meetings.

How is this different from pros/cons?

Pros/cons lists are flat. PMB shows nested arguments, tracks evolution over time, and specifically highlights critical info (!).
