The Context Trap

Important decisions happen in our heads. Even when we write things down, we lose context.

  • Why did we think that?
  • What tradeoffs did we consider?
  • What info changed our mind?

PlusMinusBang (PMB) solves this. It allows you to look back months later and remember the exact reasoning, not just the conclusion.

Visualized

Without PMB

"New job offer. 30% more money but it's a startup. Stable now but bored. Gotta decide by Friday."

Three months later:
"Why did I take this risk again?"

With PMB

Take startup offer? [Jan 15]
+ $95k → $125k (30% raise)

- Startup could fold in 12 months

! Ask: how much runway after Series A?

[Jan 18]
! Runway: 18 months IF Series A closes
Decision: Stay put
Why: Baby + mortgage = can't risk implosion

[8 months later]
! Startup folded. Dodged a bullet.

Why it matters

When you don't record reasoning, you defend choices blindly and repeat solved mistakes. Here is how PMB impacts you and your team.

The Personal Timeline

Today

You turn gut feelings into actual reasons. Force yourself to articulate why, not just what.

In 3 Weeks

Someone questions it? Simply show them. No scrambling. No guessing. Just your reasoning, right there.

Year One

You have dozens of decisions documented. You see patterns in how you weigh risk. You get better at thinking by studying yourself.

When 2 People Use It

You stop having the same conversation again and again. You stop re-explaining. You both learn from each other's reasoning.

Team Impact

Decisions come with reasoning attached. One person's mistake prevents five people from repeating it. Customer Support's reasoning on churn helps Engineering prioritize fixes a year later.

The Multiplier Effect

For every single reason captured, the value doesn't add—it multiplies. One decision captured today helps five people next month, and fifty people next year.

FAQ

Seems like more work?

Nope, helps you think faster and clearer. Also reduces repeated explanations in meetings.

How is this different from pros/cons?

Pros/cons lists are flat. PMB shows nested arguments, tracks evolution, and includes critical info.
